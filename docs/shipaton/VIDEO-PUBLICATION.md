## Title

CorkWill Log — Shipaton 2026 Next Gen Demo

## Description

I built CorkWill Log because I wanted a quick way to look back on my day. You answer a few questions, get a score that actually tells you something, and over time you can see patterns in your history. You can change the questions, the points, and the evaluation labels to fit how you think. It works in English and Japanese.

I'm entering it for the Shipaton 2026 Next Gen Award. CorkWill Log started as a web app. For Shipaton I wrapped it in Capacitor to make an iOS review build, hooked up RevenueCat's Test Store, and added an optional supporter badge for people who want to chip in. I also wrote up steps so reviewers can run it locally. Logging, history, customization, and exports are all still free.

To be clear about payments: every purchase you see goes through RevenueCat Test Store. No real money changes hands, no card is entered, there's no recurring subscription, and the app isn't on the App Store yet. The video runs on the iOS Simulator with made-up records, and I cut it down to fit the time limit. In the restore part I show a screenshot I took after a native restore went through.

Code and review instructions: [https://github.com/ryoyamag51/corkwill_log/tree/codex/shipaton-next-gen](https://github.com/ryoyamag51/corkwill_log/tree/codex/shipaton-next-gen)

Stack: TypeScript, React, vinext, Capacitor, RevenueCat, Cloudflare Workers, and D1. I used AI tools for coding and research, but I made the calls on what got built.
